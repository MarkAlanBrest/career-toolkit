Compliance support documentation
LibraryHub Mission

LibraryHub provides students, faculty, and staff with digital access to academic research, technical references, open educational resources, career preparation materials, and student support information that align with institutional programs and student learning outcomes.

Access Statement

LibraryHub is available online 24 hours a day, seven days a week, from any internet-connected device. Resources are selected to support both on-campus and online student access.

Resource Selection Policy

Resources are selected based on relevance to institutional programs, accuracy, authority, accessibility, currency, educational value, student usability, and alignment with course and program outcomes.

Annual Review Policy

LibraryHub resources should be reviewed at least annually by the LibraryHub Administrator, academic leadership, and/or program faculty. Broken, outdated, or low-value links should be updated, replaced, or removed.

Student Support

Students needing assistance using LibraryHub should contact their instructor, Student Services, or the LibraryHub Administrator. Faculty may provide classroom demonstrations or assignment-specific guidance.

Accessibility Statement

LibraryHub is designed with responsive layout, readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, semantic HTML structure, and clear labeling. Accessibility feedback should be directed to the LibraryHub Administrator.

Copyright & Acceptable Use

LibraryHub links to lawful open-access, government, institutional, and educational resources. Users are responsible for following copyright, fair use, academic integrity, and acceptable use policies.

Evidence of Use

Recommended documentation includes student orientation materials, faculty assignment examples, resource review logs, update dates, student support contacts, and analytics or usage records when available.

LibraryHub is a digital learning resource system designed to support academic research, program-specific learning, student success, career preparation, and institutional learning resources. It is not presented as a guarantee of accreditation compliance; it is designed to help document and support access to organized learning resources.
