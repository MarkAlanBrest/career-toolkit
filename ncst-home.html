New Castle School of Trades: A Skilled Trade School in Pennsylvania and Ohio

For the last 80 years, New Castle School of Trades has been helping Pennsylvania, Ohio, and West Virginia residents to get the training and hands-on expertise they need to prepare for an exciting new career in a trades field. With years of experience training skilled technicians, it has allowed NCST to develop and refine instruction methods for students while keeping our sights set firmly on the future.

TRAINING STUDENTS FOR AN EXCITING NEW CAREER IN THE TRADES INDUSTRY

NCST knows what employers in the Youngstown-Warren-Boardman, Pittsburgh, Akron, Steubenville, Ohio-Pennsylvania, Weirton, West Virginia area are looking for and what they value in an employee. Our trade programs have been structured around these needs so that when a student graduates, they will be prepared to work in the industry. A benefit of a technical school like NCST is that our hands-on training allows students to work with the same equipment used in the field of their choice, giving them an advantage during the application process.

As an experienced trade school in Pennsylvania and Ohio, New Castle School of Trades has helped thousands of people find new careers. Once an individual has determined which technical program is right for them, be assured that NCST’s commitment doesn’t end at graduation. The job placement assistance services found at NCST offers great assets for students and for employers looking to acquire skilled technicians.

We’re confident the trade school programs found at New Castle School of Trades are what you’re looking for and we’re excited to start you on a new path. Our mission at New Castle School of Trades has always stayed true. We are here to “service with excellence the needs of our community and the needs of our graduates by matching skills.”

SEPARATING OURSELVES FROM OTHER TECHNICAL SCHOOLS IN PA AND OHIO

New Castle School of Trades realizes that when it comes to vocational schools within Ohio-PA, West Virginia, and surrounding areas like Youngstown-Warren-Boardman, Pittsburgh, Weirton, and Steubenville, there are several to choose from. So, why should anyone choose the NCST in New Castle, PA, or East Liverpool, OH?

NCST is 100% dedicated to student success. New Castle understands that investing in people makes all the difference. With education, skill enhancements, and career training in some of the top technical fields, NCST can help students live their best life right now.

The close-knit community at New Castle School of Trades makes it easier for individuals to succeed in their careers because we understand the needs and goals of our students. Experienced instructors challenge trainees to develop personal responsibility and practical work skills in a hands-on format. The students at New Castle School of Trades find out first-hand that people make the difference.

When it comes down to it, the number one reason anyone should choose New Castle over other trade schools is that career training can make a positive difference in your life. Our New Castle and East Liverpool Campuses have helped thousands of other students begin a new chapter in their professional careers. Continue reading to learn more about the NCST difference along with our history and training in Pennsylvania, Ohio, and West Virginia trade students.
